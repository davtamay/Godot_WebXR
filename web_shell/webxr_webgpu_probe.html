WebXR + WebGPU probe (XRGPUBinding)

Tests whether this browser can render an immersive WebXR session through WebGPU. In the headset you should see a deep blue void in the left eye, deep green in the right, and a spinning magenta triangle fused in front of you. WebXR needs a secure context: use HTTPS, or adb reverse tcp:8060 tcp:8060 and open http://localhost:8060/... on the headset.
